Contact intake

Unverified claimed-sender inquiries. Matching and replay do not verify identity.

How this queue works

Every inquiry from the Northwind MSP website contact form arrives here first. Its ticket stays hidden from agents, automation and the client portal until a staff member verifies it.

Pending: waiting to be processed. This happens within a minute; nothing to do.
Processed: a ticket was created. Open it, check the sender is genuine, then mark it verified.
Quarantined: it could not be matched to a client. Open it and resolve it to a client, approve it as a new prospect, or keep it on hold.
