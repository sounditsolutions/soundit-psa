Settings > General (new card)

Contact intake
New web form tickets are assigned to this person. They also get the alert email for any web form inquiry whose ticket has no assignee. Only active admin and tech staff can be chosen.

Settings > Alerts Hub > route event types (two new types, routable, off until a route subscribes)

intake.webform_received: Web form inquiry received
Sent when a web form inquiry is added to a ticket (new or existing), before anyone verifies it. Carries only the submission id, ticket id, age and review link.

intake.webform_verified: Web form inquiry verified
Sent when a staff member verifies a web form inquiry and its ticket becomes visible to automation.
